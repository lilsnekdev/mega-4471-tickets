The desk closed #4471 as one known issue: the parser reads dates month first. Working the export shows three separate problems behind the 412 reports. Each needs its own ticket and its own fix; one queued fix will not cover them.

Not one issue. Three: the shared parser, the booking-v3 form hint, and a one-day move.

The three problems

  1. Parser — month-first for day-first tenants. The shared date parser reads every date month first. Every tenant whose locale writes the day first gets month and day flipped, on every channel. This is the one the desk already knows, and it is only this one the queued fix covers.
  2. Form hint — booking-v3 says MM/DD, reads day-first. On the booking-v3 form the hint under the date field says MM/DD/YYYY, so tenants type the month first — and the form then reads it the tenant's day-first way. Different bug, three tenants, form channel only. Reports of this were merged into #4471 under the same subject lines.
  3. One-day move. A handful of reports are not swaps at all: dates moved by one day, a weekday shift, an invite that arrived before the booking was made, and one meeting that showed on different days in different timezones. The desk notes read these separately; they are filed here so they stop hiding under the parser.

The numbers

Of the 412 unique reports merged into #4471 (the export carries 427 rows; 15 are duplicates), counted as the desk counts, by where each report is filed:

  • 379 reports — the parser problem. This is the desk's own counting: the 5 reports the desk reads as a one-day move are filed under parser by tenant and footnoted here, not counted apart.
  • 33 reports — the form-hint problem (tenants alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour, all via form).
  • 5 reports — the one-day move (the desk's oneDayMove notes) — a distinct problem and a distinct fix, but counted under the parser ticket above in the desk's view.

Intake's intakeTag guess disagrees with the real cause on 38 of them (23 tagged hint are parser; 15 tagged parser are hint): a browser-locale guess cannot decide this — the tenant's locale, channel and form do. Decision 0001 (merge on subject line alone) is what folded three problems into one ticket.

Report ids per problem: analysis/report-ids-by-problem.json.

Lessons

  • One ticket per problem, counted per problem. A subject line cannot tell a hint bug from a parser bug.
  • Two guesses about the same report (intake's and the tenant's) disagree; only one of them pays for the merge.
